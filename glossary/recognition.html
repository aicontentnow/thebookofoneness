RECOGNITION

10BUCKET · TERM 2/8

Direct Knowing of Oneness without seer, without Story, without Narration, without Identity. Recognition is not achieved. It is not taught. It arises when The Loop is interrupted and The Mind no longer organizes perception into Self. Recognition does not require Release, but often occurs when Release does.
